# Evenda — Google Play store listing draft

Describes only features present in the app on 20 Oct 2026. No user numbers,
testimonials or partnerships are claimed.

**App name (30 max):** Evenda
**Category:** Business
**Short description (80 max):**
Find stock, quote and book other event vendors, and keep every job on track.

**Full description:**
Evenda is built for event businesses in Zambia: tent and chair hire, décor,
sound, lighting, catering and the people who plan events.

• Find suppliers near you: search by category, dates and quantity, see who has
  stock free on your dates and how far away they are.
• Evenda Now: short of stock for an event? Send one urgent request and nearby
  suppliers who have it free can reply with offers you compare.
• Quotes and bookings: request quotations, receive revised versions, accept
  them as bookings, and record payments both sides confirm.
• Inventory that knows your dates: track stock, damaged items and returns,
  close dates, and record jobs you take outside Evenda.
• Events and tasks: plan each event, say who supplies what, and get reminders
  for the tasks that are yours.
• Supplier updates: follow suppliers and categories and hear when new stock
  is listed.
• Your location, your choice: show other vendors your exact pin or an
  approximate area.
• Verified businesses: upload your PACRA or TPIN certificate to get the
  verified badge.

Evenda records payments between vendors; it does not process or hold money.
