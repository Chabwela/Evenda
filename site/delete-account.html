Delete your Evenda account

This page is for the Evenda app by DanTech Solutions Group.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Evenda and sign in.
  2. Go to Business, tap Account & privacy, then Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: without the app

Email chabwelalembemu77@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Evenda, with the subject Delete my Evenda account. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We complete deletions within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your login, name, email and phone number; your business name, description, logo, contact details and map location; listing photos and verification documents (PACRA, TPIN, NRC); your events, tasks, reminders, follows and notifications; your push-notification tokens.

What is kept, and why

Past bookings, quotations and payment records stay in the other vendor's history, with items, dates and amounts only, and your business shown as "Deleted business", because the other party needs their own records. Records of decisions Evenda staff took on your account are kept for security and dispute handling.

Before you delete

Finish or cancel any active bookings and remove your team members (Business → Team). Evenda staff accounts are removed by a super admin.
