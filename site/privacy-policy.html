Evenda Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Evenda is a marketplace and work-planning app for event businesses in Zambia, operated by DanTech Solutions Group, Lusaka, Zambia ("Evenda", "we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Evenda app, why, who receives it, and your choices.

1. Who Evenda is for

Evenda is for businesses and their staff (vendors). It is not intended for children under 18.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other vendors on Evenda see your business profile, published listings and photos, approximate or exact location (your choice), and, for a quote or booking with them, the details of that transaction.
  • Evenda staff with a specific role (for example verification, support or finance) can see what that role needs. Their decisions are recorded in an audit log.
  • Service providers that run Evenda on our behalf:
    • Supabase (database, authentication and file storage; cloud hosting)
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications)
    • Our email delivery provider (sending sign-in and password-reset codes)
    • MapTiler (map images; the provider receives your device's IP address and the map area being viewed)
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

4. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access in our database is restricted per business, so one vendor cannot read another vendor's private data. Verification documents are stored privately and opened by staff through links that expire after 10 minutes.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it as described below, except where we must keep limited records.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Business → Account & privacy → Delete account. You can also request deletion here.

Deleted: your login, name, email and phone number; your business name, description, logo, contact details and location; listing photos and verification documents; your events, tasks, reminders, follows and notifications; your push-notification tokens.

Kept without your name: past bookings, quotations and payment records remain in the other vendor's history (items, dates and amounts) and show your business as "Deleted business", because the other party needs their own records. Records of decisions Evenda staff took on your account are kept for security and dispute handling.

Before deleting, you must finish or cancel active bookings and remove your team members.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your business information in the app at any time, choose approximate instead of exact location, turn notification types off, and delete your account. To ask about the information we hold about you, contact us below.
